01 / Scope

One project, a full numerical toolkit.

The assignment connected numerical techniques to two engineering domains: internal fluid flow and one-dimensional heat conduction.

I decoded custom floating-point data without built-in binary-conversion functions, differentiated noisy measurements, solved roots by bisection, solved linear temperature systems, compared interpolation approaches, fit regression models, integrated variable conductivity, and solved an ODE with ode45.

02 / Model judgment

Numerically valid is not automatically physical.

The high-order interpolant passed through the temperature nodes but introduced nonphysical curvature. The lower-order piecewise spline better represented the expected conduction trend.

Engineering interpretationA correct computation can still be the wrong model.

The report explicitly rejects the polynomial as a faithful physical representation and recommends the smoother piecewise spline for between-node estimates.
