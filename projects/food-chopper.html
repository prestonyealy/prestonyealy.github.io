EML 3262 collaborative project · Spring 2026

Single-Action
Food Chopper

A multi-link mechanism concept developed to reduce chopping effort and provide a repeatable guided blade path for users with limited hand strength.

  • 5+Required links
  • 1+Closed loops
  • 50%Force-reduction target
  • 38/40Report score
01 / Need

Reduce effort without losing control.

I proposed a guided chopping mechanism as one of three individual concepts; the group selected it for the semester design project.

The intended user has limited hand strength or one functional arm. A foot pedal or single downward push drives a fixed vertical blade stroke while the base remains stable.

02 / Requirements

Turn a vague idea into measurable intent.

A

Kinematic complexity

Use at least five links, one closed loop, and two different nonzero link angular velocities.

B

Effort

Target at least a 50% reduction in required force compared with a regular knife.

C

Consistency

Target repeated cut dimensions within plus or minus 2 mm.

D

Operation

Complete the cut in one smooth motion while the device remains stable.

04 / Next iteration

Build the test fixture before revising the linkage.

Recommended next version

Measure input force with a load cell, record blade displacement and link angles, run repeated cuts against the plus-or-minus-2-mm criterion, and quantify base slip and tip risk.
