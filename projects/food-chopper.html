Mechanism concept and requirement definition.

  • Developed a mechanically actuated chopping concept and described the input-to-blade motion.
  • Defined requirements covering cutting motion, operator safety, stability, cleanup, and manufacturability.
  • Evaluated the design against those requirements and documented the remaining need for quantitative kinematic analysis.
