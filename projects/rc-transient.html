Work completed

Arduino-based transient measurement.

  • Assembled the RC circuit and used an Arduino as the data-acquisition device.
  • Programmed acquisition of 500 analog samples at 50 ms intervals.
  • Plotted the measured discharge response and compared it with the nominal 10-second time constant.
  • Identified that the measured capacitor discharged faster than the nominal model predicted.
