01 / Role

Lead the test thinking.

I served as the team's Testing Engineering Lead while the group moved from candidate concepts to an integrated electromechanical prototype.

The submitted portfolio documents a requirements-weighting exercise, enclosure development, chassis integration, first testing, target specifications, and recommendations for the next revision.

02 / Architecture

Mechanical character, embedded behavior.

A

Enclosure

Multi-part PLA body, head, legs, horns, and tail designed for assembly without sharp edges.

B

Drive

Four-wheel chassis using two motors to reduce cost and weight.

C

Sensing

Ultrasonic sensing replaced an infrared approach after integration problems.

D

Control

Remote operation and obstacle avoidance were documented in the final design.

03 / Tradeoff
Two motors, one simpler system.

The team reduced motor count to improve cost and weight. The enclosure was separated into printable pieces, and the sensing approach changed when infrared performance created development problems.

A weighted-benefit analysis scored the selected design at 355, compared with 340 and 331 for the alternatives.
